Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Green Monster (developer: Studio M.) (“we”) handles information in the PatternVault app for iPhone and iPad. You do not need a PatternVault account. Optional iCloud features require an Apple Account signed in to iCloud.

For privacy questions or requests, contact [Support email — TBD]. Developer location: Japan.

At a glance

  • Files you import, capture, or create are stored in the app’s local storage.
  • The app has no advertising network, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking integration.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
  • Optional backups and shared copies are encrypted on your device before being uploaded to Apple’s iCloud services.
  • Patterns and recovery phrases are not sent to a developer-operated account server. PatternVault has no such server.

Original files can contain embedded metadata, including dates or location information added by the source camera. PatternVault does not promise to remove that metadata when importing, exporting, backing up, or sharing a file. The app does not request access to your device’s location.

Raw unlock patterns are not retained. Ordinary recovery setup stores a verifier rather than a readable recovery phrase. A new phrase generated by duress mode is temporarily retained inside the protected credential record until you acknowledge saving it.

How protection works

Local files use iOS Data Protection with complete file protection. The pattern controls access within the app; local media is not encrypted with a key derived from your pattern. The credential record has an additional layer of AES-256-GCM encryption tied to the app installation.

Encrypted iCloud backups, portable backup files, and shared archives use AES-256-GCM. Their recovery and sharing secrets serve different purposes from an unlock pattern. PatternVault covers its content in the app switcher and requires a new unlock after backgrounding.

No app can guarantee security on a compromised device, prevent every screenshot or external photograph, or retrieve a secret that you have lost. See the recovery guide for how to keep a usable backup.

Optional iCloud backup

Backup is off until you enable it for an owned vault. PatternVault uploads an encrypted archive and encrypted vault metadata to your private CloudKit database. Your recovery phrase protects the secret needed to restore the archive; the raw phrase and archive secret are not uploaded as readable fields.

Apple operates the storage service and can process service information such as account-related identifiers, record identifiers, timestamps, transfer sizes, and connection information. Encryption of the content does not conceal all service metadata. Apple’s handling is described in its Privacy Policy and iCloud terms.

Backups run while the relevant vault is open. There is one latest completed backup per configured vault, not a history of previous versions. This is separate from ordinary device backup and from iCloud Photos.

Sharing a vault

When you create a share, PatternVault makes an encrypted, read-only cloud copy for a recipient. An encrypted invitation is stored in CloudKit’s public database so another signed-in Apple Account can find it using the sharing phrase. That lookup record does not contain readable vault media or the raw phrase; account and record metadata can still be associated with it.

The person who first claims an invitation can access it through the Apple Account used for the claim. Send the phrase only to your intended recipient. A recipient can see the contents and embedded file metadata, and can export when you permit it. Even with exporting disabled, a recipient may photograph the screen or otherwise retain information they have seen.

Received content is cached locally. Opening it requires an online access check, with further checks while it remains open. Expiration, open limits, and capture controls are app rules, not a guarantee against copying. Revocation cannot remove a recipient’s separately saved copies.

Permissions and actions you control

  • Photos: the system picker supplies the items you select. Broader Photos access is requested when you choose to delete imported originals, after successful import and system confirmation. Saving or exporting through a system destination can require that destination’s permissions.
  • Camera: used when you open the private camera. Captures stay in an app-private draft until you choose Use; the camera does not automatically save them to Photos.
  • Microphone: requested for recording video with audio.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: Apple’s biometric system returns an authentication result. PatternVault does not receive your face scan, fingerprint, or biometric template.
  • Files and share sheet: PatternVault accesses selected documents or passes the files and phrases you choose to the destination you select. That destination’s handling then applies.
  • Clipboard: phrase fields have an explicit Paste control; the app does not continuously read your clipboard. Phrase-copy actions use local-only clipboard items with an expiration.

You can change system permissions in Settings. Refusing a permission limits its related feature rather than granting broader access to your content.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles payment. PatternVault uses Apple’s StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock purchased features, including product and transaction identifiers and purchase status. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app.

The current app has no custom analytics or crash-upload SDK. Apple may make app statistics and diagnostic reports available to developers according to your system settings and its policies. You can review Analytics & Improvements in device Settings; see Apple’s App Analytics & Privacy notice.

Retention and deletion

  • Local content: remains until you delete the items, delete their vault, or delete the app. Deleting a folder keeps its items in the vault. Deleting an item inside PatternVault has no in-app trash or undo.
  • Ordinary device backups: the app excludes local vault files and its installation key. Use PatternVault’s encrypted backup options if you need a restorable copy.
  • Cloud backup: choose Turn off and delete cloud backup to stop future uploads and request removal of the configured backup. The local vault remains.
  • Vault deletion: removes local content and credentials. For an owned vault, it also queues removal of its configured cloud backup and owned shares. Removing a received vault removes your local copy, not the owner’s cloud copy.
  • Pending cloud removal: deletion and revocation may wait for connectivity and the correct iCloud account. Keep PatternVault installed and reopen it to complete pending work. Deleting the app can discard pending requests.
  • App deletion: removes local files and the installation key. It does not itself delete completed iCloud backups, Photos originals, purchases, or files you exported elsewhere. Offloading the app is different: iOS keeps its documents and data.
  • External copies: manage exported files, recipient copies, and Photos originals separately. Deleting an original in Photos can affect iCloud Photos and synced devices; Photos may retain it in Recently Deleted.

Duress mode removes access credentials for other local vaults but leaves their files on disk. It is not secure erasure. It also changes recovery and backup behavior; read the duress explanation before enabling it.

Apple and any destination service apply their own retention and deletion practices to data they process, including operational records and service backups.

Support, requests, and your choices

If you contact us, we process the email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide to respond, troubleshoot, and address privacy or security concerns. We retain support information only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or a legal obligation. We may use email and support service providers to handle that correspondence and disclose information where legally required.

Please do not send unlock patterns, recovery phrases, sharing phrases, or private vault files with a support request. We do not have a server-side copy that lets us reset your pattern or decrypt a lost backup for you.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or complain to your local privacy authority. Contact [Support email — TBD] to exercise rights concerning information we hold. We may need proportionate verification. Local vault content is managed on your device; requests about Apple’s own processing should also be directed to Apple.

Children and policy updates

PatternVault does not request a date of birth or create a separate child profile. Apple Account requirements and the app’s App Store age rating apply. Where local law requires a parent or guardian’s permission, obtain it before using the relevant services. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern may contact us.

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The date at the top identifies this version. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before applying a material change. This policy concerns the app and support correspondence; additional services or tracking added to a website need their own appropriate disclosures.

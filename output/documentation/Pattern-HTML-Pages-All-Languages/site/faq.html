Basics

Do I need an account?

No Pattern account is required for local vaults. Optional iCloud backup and sharing require an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. Purchases use the App Store.

What is free, and what does Unlimited unlock?

Free use includes three owned vaults with five total items per vault across photos, videos, memos, documents, and folders. Unlimited is a one-time purchase that removes these limits and enables creating shares. Receiving shares is free and does not consume owned-vault limits. Storage capacity still depends on your device and iCloud plan.

Does each pattern open a different vault?

Yes. Each registered pattern selects its own vault. Use New vault to register another; entering an unknown pattern does not create one. A folder stays inside its parent vault and has no separate pattern.

Can I change between 3 × 3, 4 × 4, and 5 × 5?

Yes. Pattern grid in settings changes the grid for all vaults together. You must confirm every current pattern and register every replacement before saving. See the pattern and grid instructions.

Is Unlimited a subscription?

No. Both the regular purchase and a special-offer purchase are non-consumable, one-time unlocks with the same features. The price shown by Apple’s purchase flow applies. There is no recurring Pattern subscription.

Files and camera

Does importing delete my originals?

No. Pattern copies selected items. Delete originals from Photos is a separate option after successful import and requires system confirmation. It can affect iCloud Photos and synced devices. Pattern does not empty Recently Deleted. File imports do not delete source documents.

What happens to Live Photos?

The current importer saves the still image, not the motion component. Pattern therefore declines automatic deletion of those Live Photo originals. Keep the source if you need the complete Live Photo.

Will the private camera put photos in Photos?

No. Captures go into an app-private review draft. Use saves to the selected vault/folder; Retake or Cancel discards the draft. Backgrounding discards an unconfirmed capture. You can separately choose to export a saved file later.

Why is silent capture unavailable or lower quality?

Silent photos use an ARKit camera frame on supported hardware and cameras, rather than the normal full-resolution still-photo path. This can reduce detail. The speaker toggle applies to photos and does not mute video audio.

Why can’t I find an item with search?

Search matches the item or filename within the current library or folder; it does not search inside document text. Check your folder and filter. The All filter includes every item type. Changing folders or adding content clears the query.

Does deleting a folder delete its files?

No. Its items remain in the vault. Deleting selected items is a different action and permanently removes their app copies after confirmation.

Are dates or location metadata removed?

Not necessarily. Original files can retain embedded metadata, including location information supplied by the source camera. Pattern is not a metadata-removal tool; exported and shared copies may retain that information.

Security and recovery

Is everything encrypted with my pattern?

No. The pattern is an app access lock. Local files use iOS complete file protection, and the credential record has separate installation-bound encryption. Cloud and portable backup archives are encrypted with AES-256-GCM; their encryption is not derived from the unlock pattern.

What if I forget my pattern?

Choose Recover vault and use the recovery phrase to set a replacement for that vault. If neither credential is available, support cannot bypass the lock for you. A usable encrypted backup and its original phrase can be restored as a separate vault.

Can a recovery phrase restore files after deleting the app?

Only if a completed cloud backup or a saved .patternbackup archive also exists. A phrase does not contain your files. Local vaults are excluded from ordinary device backups; reinstalling or restoring a purchase does not bring those files back.

Which phrase opens an older backup?

An exported archive always uses the phrase from when it was created. After you change a recovery phrase, an existing cloud copy continues to use its previous phrase until the next backup completes. The new phrase applies to local recovery immediately.

Why did Face ID or Touch ID open a different vault?

When configured, Pattern deliberately opens your chosen dummy vault if biometrics fail, are cancelled, or are unavailable. This alone does not activate duress or change backups. A system interruption leaves the app locked. Unlock the dummy with its own pattern for its full settings.

What does duress actually do?

Unlocking with an armed duress pattern keeps the selected dummy, or the triggering vault if there is no dummy, and removes access credentials for all other local vaults. It rotates the survivor’s recovery phrase, stops backup/sharing, and queues owned-share revocations. Files for revoked vaults remain on disk. Disabling duress does not undo it. Read the full consequences before enabling it.

Can Pattern prevent screenshots?

Not completely. For a shared vault with capture locking enabled, the app can react to capture detection and lock access. It cannot prevent the first screenshot or someone photographing the screen with another camera.

Backup and sharing

Does iCloud keep all my devices automatically in sync?

No. Personal cloud backup runs while the relevant vault is open and stores the latest completed copy. Restoration makes an independent local vault. It is not bidirectional device sync and does not maintain historical backup versions.

Why isn’t my backup finishing?

Check internet access, available iCloud/device storage, and that you are signed in to the Apple Account used to set it up. Keep the vault open, tap Back up now, and check the completion date. Reopen to retry interrupted uploads. Record an error message for support, but do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step before verifying a backup.

Why is the restore list empty?

You must use the Apple Account that owns a backup, and at least one upload must have completed. Ordinary device backup, iCloud Photos, and exported files in iCloud Drive are different. For a .patternbackup file, choose Import a backup file instead.

Can I open a received vault offline?

No. It requires online access checks before opening and periodically while open. Your own local vaults can be used offline. Exporting from a received vault additionally depends on the owner’s permission.

Is a sharing phrase the same as a recovery phrase?

No. Send only the generated sharing phrase to an intended recipient. The first claiming Apple Account becomes the recipient; the same account can import again using that phrase. Your unlock pattern and recovery phrase should remain private.

Does revocation delete everything the recipient has seen?

No. It prevents further access through the share after iCloud receives the revocation and access is rechecked. It cannot erase exported copies, existing screenshots, or external photographs. Keep Pattern installed until pending revocations complete.

Is the open limit enforced across all installations?

No. Open counts are maintained per local installation and survive normal relaunches. Removing the received vault or reinstalling can reset the local counter. Treat this as an app convenience, not protection against a determined recipient.

How do I delete a cloud backup?

In that vault’s iCloud backup settings, choose Turn off and delete cloud backup. It removes the configured cloud copy when iCloud is available and keeps local files. Deleting the app alone does not remove completed cloud backups. See the deletion details.

Appearance, purchases, and help

Does changing the icon also rename the app?

No. The installed app stays named Pattern. The App icon screen includes a guide for creating a separately named Home Screen shortcut through Apple Shortcuts. That does not hide or rename the installed app.

How do I restore Unlimited?

Open Pattern Unlimited in settings and choose Restore purchases while using the Apple Account that purchased it. Apple may ask you to sign in. This restores the license, not vault files; use a cloud or file backup to restore content.

How do I request a refund?

For an App Store purchase, follow Apple’s refund process. Eligibility depends on the purchase, country or region, and applicable law. A refunded entitlement can remove Unlimited, while the current app preserves access to existing local content and limits new additions.
